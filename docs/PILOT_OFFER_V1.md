# MatVerse Trust Runtime — Controlled Enterprise Pilot

## What it is

A bounded enterprise pilot for organizations testing AI agents or AI-driven workflows that need explicit authorization, policy enforcement, evidence and replay.

## What the pilot does

For one selected workflow, MatVerse Trust Runtime creates a governed path:

`request -> authenticated principal -> frozen contract -> admissibility -> controlled action boundary -> evidence receipt -> ledger -> replay`

The customer can inspect what was requested, which contract governed it, what decision was made, and which observable evidence was retained.

## Best-fit customers

- companies deploying internal AI agents;
- fintech, insurance and regulated software teams;
- AI platforms and agent builders;
- security, compliance and audit teams;
- R&D organizations that need reproducible AI-assisted workflows.

## Recommended first use cases

Choose one narrow, measurable process such as:

- approval of an AI agent action before access to an internal system;
- governed handoff between two AI runtimes;
- policy enforcement for sensitive-data handling;
- auditable AI-generated operational recommendation;
- controlled execution with receipt and replay.

## Pilot scope

Typical pilot target: one workflow, one customer domain, one or two AI/runtime participants, a frozen policy/contract bundle, a predefined scenario set and a final evidence review.

The pilot intentionally avoids broad autonomous deployment. It is designed to prove or falsify fit on a bounded workflow before expansion.

## Customer receives

- scoped architecture and integration plan;
- configured governed runtime;
- customer-specific policy/contract package;
- authenticated integration;
- scenario execution;
- evidence receipts and ledger export;
- replay report;
- findings and expansion recommendation.

## Success criteria

A successful pilot demonstrates that the selected workflow can be executed or blocked according to the agreed policy while leaving reconstructable evidence of the observable decision path.

## Expansion after pilot

A successful pilot can move to:

1. private deployment;
2. annual enterprise license;
3. additional governed workflows;
4. EvidenceOS retention / witness services;
5. Bridge and Federation integration;
6. OEM or embedded licensing where appropriate.
